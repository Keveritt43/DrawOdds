# Trophy tiers: direct draw-odds and cutoff review

The owner asked that fewer tags not by themselves improve trophy scoring. Non-Idaho trophy rules no longer use observed draw odds to cap a tier, nor Colorado or Wyoming preference cutoffs to promote one. Draw odds and points still affect the separately labeled Opportunity and draw information. The model still uses comparable raw application counts as a demand signal; those counts are not animal measurements and can themselves be affected by regulation, opportunity and hunter behavior. Provisional source signals remain provisional.

The existing Idaho table and older Idaho rules are unchanged here: their stored score columns require a coordinated, stale-protected scores-only update. The existing non-Idaho seasonal tier adjustment also remains a larger influence than the owner requested. This change is a correction to two inputs, not completion of the wider scoring rollout.

On the checked-in current state snapshots, comparing the previous resolver against this one changes 0 of 1,565 WA deer/elk/pronghorn hunts, 7 of 601 Utah hunts, 4 of 656 Montana hunts and 11 of 520 Wyoming hunts. These are hunt-level estimates from retained data; they are not a fresh production database dry run. Utah Zion general deer DB1589 moves Standard to Good rather than being capped based on its draw rate. Montana elk permits 270-45 and 410-21 move from Standard to provisional Elite as the remaining named-source and demand rules no longer get suppressed by the high-odds screen; these signals do not establish trophy size. Wyoming Area 38 Type 2 pronghorn moves Excellent to Good when its point-cutoff promotion is removed. Review exact changes and source scope; score and Quality may differ on the live site when the inputs differ.

Colorado hunt summaries are stored in the database and are recomputed on read. The public export does not include complete point-pool inputs, so a full Colorado before/after count was not established here. Representative matching and nonmatching cutoff tests confirm that changing a cutoff or the number awarded does not change the Colorado trophy tier. No agency data was imported, and historical database records were not rewritten.
