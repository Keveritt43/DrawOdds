# Idaho trophy: demand mix, own-hunt antler data, same-unit rut ordering, no hunter minimum (2026-11-24)

Owner decisions in chat, Nov 24, 2026. Idaho first; the same rules then go to the other states one at a time, each with its own rescore.

- **Demand = half raw applications, half applications per tag**, each a mid-rank percentile within species among the 236 table hunts (weight 35). The owner first asked for raw applications only, then for a ratio as well once raw counts put 300-tag 1042 level with 20-tag 1043. Draw odds are not added on top: in Idaho's random draw odds are about tags / applications, so it would count scarcity twice. In point states the per-tag ratio is still the right measure, since draw odds there depend on the hunter's points.
- **Antler share comes from each hunt's own harvest**, pulled toward the species mean with a 20-animal prior (no minimum). Only a hunt with no harvest of its own borrows its weakest unit's best single-unit figure. Before this, every hunt in a unit got the unit's best hunt's share: 1042 (Oct, 300 tags, own 52% 4+ points over 890 deer) was scored on 1043's 86% (Nov, 20 tags).
- **Timing is small** (5 of 100), but **within the same units, weapon and deer type, the deer or elk hunt with more rut days ranks above the earlier one** (`orderByRut`, raises the rut hunt 0.1 above the best earlier hunt; never lowers one). Deer window corrected from Nov 1–15 to all of November (Nov 10–30 hunts had been credited less than Oct 17–Nov 9).
- **No hunter minimum** for Quality (`src/lib/score.ts`, all states' code; previously 30 hunters, 10 for moose/sheep/goat/bison). Success is already pulled toward a conservative baseline with a 50-hunter prior (10 for once-in-a-lifetime species), and cards still say "Small sample". Stored scores in other states change only at their next import or rescore.
- Weights (`LIVE_WEIGHTS`): demand 35, mature-antler share 47, named sources 8, county B&C 5, timing 5. Tiers by rank within species: top 2% Elite, next 8% Excellent, next 30% Good.

Result on the retained 2026-11-16 snapshot (`scripts/build-idaho-noquota-tiers.ts`): 236 scored; 5 Elite, 19 Excellent, 70 Good, 142 Standard; 78 tiers differ from the live table. Unit 54: 1043 rank 1 (Elite), 1042 rank 51 (Standard). Others: 1049 rank 2 (Elite), 1044 rank 3 (Excellent), 1031 rank 6 and 1034 rank 11 (Excellent), 1057 rank 92 (Standard). Lifted by the rut rule: deer 1005 > 1004, 1003 > 1002; elk 2033 > 2115, 2019 > 2020.

Other states not changed yet: their tiers use unit evidence, raw applications (95th percentile) and drop a whole tier outside the preferred season. Port these rules next.
