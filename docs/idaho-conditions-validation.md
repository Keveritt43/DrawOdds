# Idaho conditions and access-site validation

November 22, 2026 snapshot. The source downloads are public, cached in `out/id-conditions`, and fingerprinted in `data/id-conditions.json`. The build requires 100 Idaho units and checks all agency query object IDs. The bounding box returned 1,336 Forest Service trailhead and 139 BLM records; 577 unique records fall in an Idaho unit. The BLM layer includes access points and staging areas as well as trailheads. No conclusion about hunter counts, closure status or public access follows from these totals.

NRCS returned 87 active Idaho station records; 91 unit centroids have a station within 35 miles. Values are same-station, same-date measurements in inches of snow-water equivalent. For example, Unit 54's nearest station is Magic Mountain, 5.7 miles from its center: April 1 SWE was 29.0 inches in 2023 and 0.3 inches in 2026. Station measurements are not a unit-wide snow measure or survival estimate. Current readings preserve the actual date and are provisional. NRCS's 1991–2020 median is only compared for the same calendar day.

US Drought Monitor polygons are unioned at D1+ and intersected with IDFG unit polygons, with year and date retained. The pinned November 17 archive has the same SHA-256 as the current archive downloaded on November 22. Every reported unit percentage is bounded by 0–100. Historical samples are the last Tuesday in September, rather than an annual or growing-season average. Weather observations and access-site counts do not alter scores. Neither source data sync nor a database or schema change is part of this release.
