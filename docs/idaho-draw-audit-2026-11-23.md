# Idaho general draw and scoring check — 2026-11-23

Compared the production planner's public Idaho tag rows with the [2027 IDFG nonresident supplement](https://idfg.idaho.gov/sites/default/files/seasons-rules-big-game-supplemental-proclamation-2027.pdf), pages 10–15. All 192 published general deer and elk hunt codes were present, with no quota mismatches or extra codes. This is the general draw, not controlled hunts, DAV or outfitter allocations. The 2027 seasons were not yet set; the site retains 2026 season information as historical context.

Compared 138 deer codes' 2026 tag, first-choice applicant and award counts against readable rows in IDFG's [2026 draw report](https://idfg.idaho.gov/sites/default/files/nonresident-draw-data-and-descriptions_2026-draw.pdf); all 138 matched. This was not an exhaustive validation of every choice or the elk draw. Earlier-choice awards and group applications affect who remains eligible for later choices. The report's published later-choice counts do not determine the chance for a particular choice combination. The planner now reports those counts as historical results and does not combine them into a personal overall chance or expected tag expenditure. Missing denominators are unknown.

The public planner endpoint returned 143 adult controlled deer, 164 elk and 49 pronghorn hunts. Stored Quality was missing for 19 deer, 7 elk and 2 pronghorn. The retained November 23 score snapshot provides the reasons: 21 have fewer than the model's required 30 participating hunters, six lack usable hunter/harvest data, and deer 1044 has intentionally withheld land and density because its former two-unit geography was incorrect. The 21 small samples cannot be filled under the present model; the six missing histories require source review; 1044 needs independently verified hunt-area inputs. These counts cover this endpoint's adult controlled hunts and do not describe general hunts or all stored rows.

The Idaho trophy rollout was verified separately on November 23 using a production snapshot: 1,303 summary rows were checked after the scores-only apply. See [the rollout record](idaho-scoring-rollout.md). This audit did not repeat a full production score snapshot, independently validate every trophy source, or change scoring or production data.
