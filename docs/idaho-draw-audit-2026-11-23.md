# Idaho general draw and scoring check — 2026-11-23

Compared the production planner's public Idaho tag rows with the [2027 IDFG nonresident supplement](https://idfg.idaho.gov/sites/default/files/seasons-rules-big-game-supplemental-proclamation-2027.pdf), pages 10–15. All 192 published general deer and elk hunt codes were present, with no quota mismatches or extra codes. This is the general draw, not controlled hunts, DAV or outfitter allocations. The 2027 seasons were not yet set; the site retains 2026 season information as historical context.

Compared 138 deer codes' 2026 tag, first-choice applicant and award counts against readable rows in IDFG's [2026 draw report](https://idfg.idaho.gov/sites/default/files/nonresident-draw-data-and-descriptions_2026-draw.pdf); all 138 matched. This was not an exhaustive validation of every choice or the elk draw. Earlier-choice awards and group applications affect who remains eligible for later choices. The report's published later-choice counts do not determine the chance for a particular choice combination. The planner now reports those counts as historical results and does not combine them into a personal overall chance or expected tag expenditure. Missing denominators are unknown.

The public planner endpoint returned 143 adult controlled deer, 164 elk and 49 pronghorn hunts. At the time of the snapshot, stored Quality was missing for 19 deer, 7 elk and 2 pronghorn: 21 had fewer than the model's required 30 participating hunters, six lacked usable hunter/harvest data, and deer 1044 had land and density withheld because its former geography was incorrect. Area 55 was subsequently independently verified and the score for 1044 restored; the 21 small samples remain below the present model's threshold. These counts cover this endpoint's adult controlled hunts and do not describe general hunts or all stored rows.

The Idaho trophy rollout was verified separately on November 23 using a production snapshot: 1,303 summary rows were checked after the scores-only apply. A later Area 55 scores-only correction changed three scores and verified all Idaho non-score fields and other-state summaries unchanged. See [the rollout record](idaho-scoring-rollout.md). This audit did not independently validate every trophy source.

The [2026 Idaho regulations](https://idfg.idaho.gov/sites/default/files/seasons-rules-big-game-2026.pdf) list deer hunt 1074 as Area 41-1 and define that area as all of Units 41 and 42. The stored summary lists only Unit 41. The site corrects the 2026 identity on read and withholds area-derived access and density; it does not change stored historical data. IDFG's [hunt planner](https://idfg.idaho.gov/ifwis/huntplanner/hunt/82192) links 1074 to prior hunt 1076, with 2022–25 totals of 71 hunters and 12 harvested deer; these records are not yet attached on the site. Agency area labels have varied and the agency's area acreage differs from the sum of the site's unit acreages, so area-based density and access still require reconciliation.

The [2025 elk harvest table](https://idfg.idaho.gov/ifwis/huntplanner/stats/?game=elk&season=controlled&yr=2025) reports 208 hunters and 57 elk for 2133, 61 and 31 for 2281, and 73 and 34 for 2282. The 2025 regulations' correction to hunt 2133 and the relevant 2025 and 2026 regulations support further work to attach these one-year histories to their corresponding current hunts. This has not yet been implemented or released. Do not infer a trophy-size breakdown from the 2133 total: published component estimates do not sum exactly to the total. Hunts 2280 and 2285 changed area or hunt conditions; do not attach their older observations solely because the hunt number matches. The remaining history corrections require consistent display, filtering, exports and a fresh protected score review before production use.
