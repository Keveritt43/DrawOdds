# Utah onboarding: research starting point

Use this worksheet with the reusable [state-onboarding checklist](../state-onboarding-checklist.md). This is a preliminary inventory, not a completed integration or permission to import production data. Reverify current agency rules, report coverage, access terms and available years for each release; record evidence and unresolved gaps.

## Agency sources

- [Big game drawing odds and point reports](https://wildlife.utah.gov/biggame/odds) and the [big game hub](https://wildlife.utah.gov/biggame): inventory actual historical and newer reporting formats, years, hunt codes, applicant pools and residency categories. DWR's hub distinguishes odds and point reports before and from 2026; verify the actual formats and coverage rather than assuming continuity.
- [Bonus and preference point guidance](https://wildlife.utah.gov/licenses/points): investigate the point system for each permit and species. DWR distinguishes bonus points from preference points, including preference points for specified general-season and antlerless hunts. Check current rules, draw phases, eligibility and choices before modeling; do not apply a single split across all permits.
- [Big game annual reports](https://wildlife.utah.gov/hunting/reports) and [harvest and survey data](https://wildlife.utah.gov/biggame/reports): inventory species, reporting periods, hunt and unit granularity, survey methods, available counts and limitations. Verify the underlying downloadable evidence and whether comparable hunters-afield denominators exist.
- [Hunt tables, maps and boundaries](https://wildlife.utah.gov/maps): research hunt-specific boundaries and legal descriptions, geometry access, attribution, update cadence and differences between hunt areas and reporting units. Confirm land-ownership and access sources separately.
- [Guidebooks](https://wildlife.utah.gov/guidebooks) and [big game hub](https://wildlife.utah.gov/biggame): verify current regulations, dates, fees, license prerequisites and eligibility; identify the applicable year and incomplete costs before displaying totals.

## Utah-specific work to verify

- [ ] Inventory deer, elk, pronghorn, moose, bighorn sheep and mountain goat opportunities and source coverage by year and hunt type; distinguish available source data from finished app support.
- [ ] Document and test preference and bonus point rules, pools, resident/nonresident eligibility, choice order, allocation phases and permit-specific exceptions against current official guidance. Build a Utah-specific odds adapter and validate representative results; label historical observations, assumptions and projections separately.
- [ ] Reconcile hunt codes, seasons and boundaries with harvest and draw reports, including unit changes and broad-area data. Record source years and unknowns; do not assign an entire regional count as a local observation for each unit.
- [ ] Validate harvest success, density, access and scoring inputs and species-specific benchmarks. Preserve agency estimates and survey limitations; leave unsupported fields and scores unknown.
- [ ] Complete the shared checklist for database identity, personalized settings, shared and Utah-specific filters, maps, cards, details, both UAT personas, performance and protected rollout. Record checks and remaining gaps before enabling Utah.
