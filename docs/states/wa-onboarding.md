# Washington onboarding: research starting point

Use this worksheet with the reusable [state-onboarding checklist](../state-onboarding-checklist.md). This is a preliminary inventory, not a completed integration or permission to import production data. Reverify current agency rules, report coverage, access terms and available years for each release; record evidence and unresolved gaps.

## Agency sources

- [Special hunt permits](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/special-hunts) and [drawing result summaries](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/special-hunts/results): inventory reports, hunt categories, application choices, point levels, residency and years. Verify what the published statistics actually support before calculating personalized odds.
- [Game harvest reports](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/management/game-harvest) and [harvest reporting](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/requirements/harvest-reporting): inventory species, years, units, general and special hunts, reporting methods, estimates and revisions. Do not combine overlapping general, special and combined tables without reconciling their populations.
- [Game management units](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/locations/gmu): identify official GMU and hunt-area boundaries, open data, legal descriptions, version, licensing and attribution. Verify how special hunt areas relate to GMUs before joining data.
- [Hunting regulations](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/regulations) and [special hunt permits](https://wdfw.wa.gov/hunting/special-hunts): verify current license prerequisites, eligibility, dates, weapon and species restrictions, fees and application rules. Record applicable years and incomplete costs.

## Washington-specific work to verify

- [ ] Inventory actual supported deer, elk, moose, bighorn sheep and mountain goat opportunities and source coverage by year and hunt type. Verify whether any proposed species or permits exist before showing them; availability of a report is not completed app support.
- [ ] Verify special-permit point and drawing rules directly in current WDFW regulations, including how points are counted, weighted, earned and reset; application choices, party rules and eligibility. Do not assume an unverified squared-points formula or a draw process from another state. Validate representative published results and label estimates and limitations.
- [ ] Reconcile hunt codes, dates, special permit categories, GMUs and boundaries with harvest and draw reports, including changes over time. Preserve agency estimates and survey limitations; retain unknown or suppressed data rather than filling it with zero.
- [ ] Validate harvest, density, land access and species-specific Quality inputs. Distinguish a broader-area estimate from a local count, and keep unsupported values or scores unknown.
- [ ] Complete the shared checklist for state-qualified database identity, residency and points, shared and Washington-only filters, maps, cards, details, both UAT personas, performance and protected rollout. Record checks and remaining gaps before enabling Washington.
