# Idaho mapped-route and elevation pilot: validation

Snapshot built 2026-11-22 UTC from public service queries. Processing uses approximately 100-m EPSG:5070 raster cells. Each value is the count of qualifying public-management cells, multiplied by their cell area. Roads and trails are buffered 1,609.344 metres before rasterization; elevation is sampled in metres and 9,000 feet equals 2,743.2 metres. Route buffers include features outside the unit bounding box by at least the buffer distance. All four reported acres are measured on the same grid. These are approximate unit-wide screening figures and are not hunt-boundary acres, legal access, or actual hunter-pressure estimates.

- IDFG returned 100 unique game management unit names. The land layer returned 15,622 records. A second download ordered by unique object ID contained exactly 15,622 distinct IDs, and its management category and geometry fingerprints matched the original download. Each unit has at least 98% surface-management or identified water coverage.
- The agency route services exhibited a projection-dependent spatial-query problem: feature pages did not always match the ID list returned for the same envelope, and some pages repeated or omitted records. The final inputs are corrected to exactly the queried object-ID sets, with missing features fetched by ID and unexpected/duplicate features discarded. `data/id-terrain-provenance.json` records counts and SHA-256 digests of the unit-level inputs and elevation rasters. Route inventory and legal status remain incomplete regardless of record-count validation.
- Unit 54: IDFG reports 34.59% USFS, 22.79% BLM and 1.85% state management, or 59.23% combined. The pilot's 517,677 acres divided by the 874,033-acre mapped unit is 59.23%. This validates a representative land overlay, not the remoteness estimate. https://idfg.idaho.gov/ifwis/huntplanner/unit.aspx?ID=69
- Unit 76 initially had 96.0% land-layer coverage. USGS NHD's Bear Lake polygon accounts for 34,646 of the approximately 35,575 uncovered acres; incorporating the lake for coverage and excluding it from public land yields 99.90% coverage. The NHD polygon's entire lake is 283.31 sq km, consistent with IDFG's 69,446-acre figure. https://idfg.idaho.gov/ifwis/fishingPlanner/water/1113174420681
- As an independent geometry check, 40 seeded Unit 30A public cells above 9,000 feet were compared with direct nearest-line distances from the verified source geometries: 20 were beyond one mile and 20 were within it; all 40 agreed with the buffered-grid classification. IDFG describes Unit 30A as high mountain habitat in the Beaverhead Mountains: https://idfg.idaho.gov/sites/default/files/plan-moose.pdf. This does not validate the completeness or current legal status of route inventories.
- The final artifact contains 100 units. All observed remote-high values are no greater than the corresponding remote or high values, and neither exceeds public-managed area. Unit 54 has no measured public cells above 9,000 ft; Unit 30A has 9,254 acres satisfying both conditions. Unit 76's joint estimate is only 30 acres, so the UI uses “<0.1 sq mi” rather than rounding it to zero. These are reproducibility and arithmetic checks, not independent ground verification of every route.

The USGS road inventory and agency motorized-route layers can omit routes or include closed, seasonal or private roads. BLM route classifications are often unknown. Elevation source resolution and date vary. These uncertainties can change the result in either direction, and the model is not used in Quality or Opportunity. No production data import, schema operation, score update or automatic sync is involved.
