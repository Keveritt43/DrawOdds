# Colorado elk and pronghorn preset review

Colorado's season resolver previously required three source, county-record and demand signals for Elite, but the county tier threshold excluded all elk hunts in the retained Colorado snapshot. A provisional Elite is now available for regular-season elk hunts in units 2, 10 and 201 only when their exact 2026 hunt code, unit and application count match the top quartile in a comparable weapon/season group of at least 30 hunts. The published unit forecasts are not measurements of the animal harvested and do not establish independent data sources. Private-land, youth and special-season codes do not inherit this rule. Existing restrictions, multi-unit floor and harvest screens still apply.

Evidence: [GOHUNT 2026 Colorado elk forecast](https://www.gohunt.com/browse/application-strategies/application-strategy-colorado-elk-and-antelope-draw-strategies-plus-tactics-to-help-you-apply), [Huntin' Fool Colorado elk overview](https://www.huntinfool.com/colorado-elk-hunting), and the project's 2026 Colorado season-demand observations. In the retained 2,038-hunt Colorado snapshot, five single-unit regular hunts meet the new rule: EE002O1A, EE201O1A, EM002O1M, EM010O1M and EM201O1M. These are historical inputs and may differ from live data.

Pronghorn peak-rut timing uses a September 11-30 midpoint window, a screening approximation consistent with the owner-supplied date table, [Montana FWP's broad rut window](https://fwp.mt.gov/binaries/content/assets/fwp/hunt/faqs-from-montana-hunters-2023.pdf), and [Wyoming Game and Fish](https://wgfd.wyo.gov/wyoming-wildlife/wyoming-wildlife-magazine/american-pronghorn-species-profile). Book-entry volumes are affected by season dates and hunting effort and do not by themselves establish a biological peak or probability of trophy harvest. Local timing varies.
